Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers Zero to Payout Uploader (“the app”), a private tool run by the owner of the Zero to Payout YouTube channel.

What the app does

The app uploads videos made by the channel owner to the channel owner's own YouTube channel, sets their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, schedules their publish time, and reads back the status of those uploads.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token for the YouTube account the owner chooses. It uses only these permissions: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and reading the owner's own channel and video status (youtube.readonly).

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. It does not collect, sell or share any personal information, and it does not access any other person's account or data.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also ends the app's access.
